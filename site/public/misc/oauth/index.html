rclone sync — personal Google Drive client

This page is the home page of a personal, single-user OAuth client used to run rclone between one Google Drive account and one computer. It is not a product, has no other users, and is published only because Google requires a home page, a privacy policy and terms of service for OAuth clients in production status.

It is unrelated to Aglaïa, which merely hosts these pages.
